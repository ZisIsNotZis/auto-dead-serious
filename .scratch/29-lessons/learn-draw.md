## Top 5 by value

1. **Verify the delivered artifact came through the named seam** (`.scratch/35-r1-articulated-study/issues/20-execution-loop-audit.md`): a whole cycle plus fresh reviews were spent on an SVG that bypassed the required adapter while every local check and manifest claim passed — the general policy has no rule that a claimed provenance path is asserted on the artifact, not on the ticket.
2. **Gate on the artifact the user consumes** (`.scratch/20-ribbon-drawing-rule/outcome.md`): XML validity, path equality, file presence and unit tests all passed on a batch that rendered blank; a gate that reads the intermediate source instead of the raster is the cheapest possible false PASS.
3. **A gate must prove it can see before its verdict counts** (`principles.md#P26`, `rubric.md`): reviewers silently returned "model does not support images" and produced verdict-shaped objects; the project's own mitigation — a pre-registered sighted fact with its answer already measured — belongs in the general policy next to `fresh_reviewer`.
4. **The ratchet: progress is measured against your own best artifact, never against your own design** (`principles.md#P23`, `AGENTS.md` invariant 8): "FINAL IMAGE" artifacts landed 4.5–5.6x below the best drawing already in the repo because nothing compared them; `baseline_first` covers the baseline, not the regression alarm on every later revision.
5. **A worker never rolls its own cap** (`issues/26`, `issues/16`, `issues/20`): a 45-minute cap overran by 11m01s and the checkpoint was missed, and the audit shows repeated continuation authorizations after exhaustion — the policy needs the deadline as an authority boundary, with breach recorded plainly and a new allocation naming what it supersedes.
