## Findings

- [pitfall] m1/failures.md#F-040 - "校验器长期空转": the validator's root call `checkNode(model,'',[])` was deleted by an unrelated diff at commit `6740f1c`, so every node-level check (unknown keys, `_intent`, `_primary`, `_when`, `_expose`, `_rel`) was dead code; `{"_intent":"x","_bogus":1}` returned `ok`, and all "42/42 models validated" claims since 2026-09-28 were "空真" (vacuously true) - policy candidate (a gate that has never been shown to fail is not a gate; ship a self-test with bad samples or the claim is empty).
- [pitfall] m1/failures.md#F-041 - restoring the root call exposed 9 real model defects (5 dangling root `_primary`, 4 `_primary` written as objects, 1 out-of-enum `_rel.order`); they were repaired by **deleting** the invalid declaration, explicitly "不改成能解析但语义错的引用", and the repair was proven render-neutral by per-file byte comparison (9/9 identical) - policy candidate (repair invalid data by deletion and prove zero behavioural change, instead of inventing a plausible replacement).
- [pitfall] m1/failures.md#F-042 - entry-level `_action.when` is never evaluated (`renderControls`/`itemExtra` read `it._action` directly), so antd's "Delete selected" with `when: role == "admin"` renders with `role` never injected: "条件门形同虚设" - policy candidate (an unenforced declaration is worse than no declaration; the deny-or-allow default when context is missing must be decided before the fix).
- [pitfall] m1/failures.md#F-052 + .scratch/13-metric-honesty/report.md - the fidelity denominator silently omitted `_fields` money/list values, so adding real content made the metric greener (aws 81→155, vm-sizes 37→196, library total 3739→3972) and the report had to state "本轮数字没有下降" to stay honest; the fix unified the walk and the rule is "口径只加严不放宽" - policy candidate (a metric whose scope one implementer can narrow silently will be narrowed; the fix is to make tightening the only allowed direction and say who owns the scope).
- [pitfall] m1/failures.md#F-054 - fidelity used plain substring containment, so deleting two `$0` cells from a rendered page still reported 1.00 ("旧口径仍报 1.00（盲）"); the repaired check uses visible text plus original-offset boundary rules and reports 0.987 naming the two paths, with 10 new counterexample assertions - policy candidate (every check needs a live counterexample where it fails; "gate 不会失败就不是 gate").
- [pitfall] m1/failures.md#F-078 (second entry, "保真度量只测模型↔渲染，不测模型↔抓取") - `aliyun-com/product-ecs` prices were `1238`/`1385` in the model against `￥1438.68/1年` in the capture, and fidelity stayed 1.00 through every round; "F-025（抓取即真相）此前只靠流程纪律保证，没有门禁"; the added `capture-integrity.mjs` (with a reasoned allowlist: 37 conversions + 4 declared capture gaps) immediately found 3 real curation errors in `linear-app/home` - policy candidate (a claim of provenance needs a check against the source, not against internal consistency).
- [pitfall] m1/failures.md#F-025 - a curator fabricated 3 URLs by combining a brand name read off the screenshot with a guessed path; they are absent from the capture DOM; adopted rule: "URL 必须逐字来自 capture...截图可见但 capture 无背书的事实 → 从 capture 补证或丢弃" - policy candidate (name the artefacts that count as evidence for a fact; a picture is not one).
- [know-how] m1/failures.md#F-075 - a page-tail band was rendered as content although the capture's own pixels were pure white (dark-pixel ratio 0.000, re-crop produced "空集"): the source's lazy-loaded block was never painted, so the blank was the source's own hole; handling was remove-the-band + log a capture gap, never synthesise, and "不得从截图转写" - policy candidate (a capture is a snapshot of a moment; an empty region is absence, not content to be backfilled).
- [know-how] m1/failures.md#F-035 / #F-036 / #F-075-3 - three instances of one rule the project learned: grafana panels were captured as "Loading plugin panel" with 0 numeric nodes, taobao hall products exist only as 0-byte images, mi15's 20 long images were never painted - in each case the model records what was captured and the gap is logged, because loading text and in-image text are not facts - policy candidate (make "the capture was in a transient state" a first-class recorded gap).
- [pitfall] m1/failures.md#F-037 + #F-038 - a fix round introduced its own regression: the role unit was appended on top of the model's own unit ("870 人好评 条评价", ~20 places) plus a duplicated H2; the next independent verification round judged FAIL and caught it, and each recurring defect class then earned a permanent check (`unit-stack`, `dup-title` in `m1/eval/audit-visual.mjs`) - policy candidate (a fix needs its own regression proof, and every defect class that recurs gets a standing check rather than a one-off repair).
- [pitfall] m1/failures.md#F-070 - the committed gallery PNGs were older than the HTML they showed (52 s, reproduced independently by four reviewers); the user judges those images, so the drift read as "not fixed"; the repair binds PNG to HTML by sha256+mtime in `renders/manifest.json`, runs `--check-shots` in the default pipeline, and makes renderer writes idempotent (otherwise the freshness assertion is always false) - policy candidate, extends `bind_to_revision` (hash-bind the derived artefact the human actually looks at, and make the writer idempotent or the freshness check can never pass).
- [pitfall] m1/failures.md#F-081 - image attribution mis-assigned 7 of 11 planned images because the DOM tokeniser `continue`d on closing tags, so the ancestor stack never popped and "parent" meant "nearest still-open `<a>`"; it was found only because the planned insertions were rejected with counter-evidence, and the promoted scanner now refuses to emit any mapping when `dangling-open-tags != 0` - policy candidate (a nesting tracker must fail loudly on unbalanced input rather than guess a parent).
- [pitfall] .scratch/active-work.md#⚠-重大发现 and #编排事故 - commit `162ef7e` is labelled as the round-11 capture but contains round-10 in-flight work, because the parent ran `git add -A` while an `m1/**` writer was running; the new rule recorded is "有 m1/** 写入者运行时不得 git add -A，只提交明确路径或等其结束" - policy candidate (staging is a shared resource: an unscoped `add -A` seizes another writer's unfinished work).
- [pitfall] .scratch/active-work.md#编排教训（2026-10-01，两次超时后总结） - three measured overruns: a single 100-minute repair round always timed out (→ 40-minute slices), an acceptance agent that read 8+ whole-page screenshots produced only a skeleton after 45 minutes (→ hard limit ≤4 images per review, `convert -crop` for detail), and a capture agent that read tool source code captured zero pages in 35 minutes (→ evaluate at most 3 candidates then capture); mitigations are "报告骨架先写、每完成一项立刻落盘" and budgets by task type (image/capture 20-25 min, code 40 min) - policy candidate (budget by task type, land each item as it completes, and cap the expensive perception step).
- [detour] m1/failures.md#F-023 - the assumed cause of a truncated-looking title was the capture's `MAX_TEXT=1000`; the real cause was capture during a typewriter animation, and the complete string was already present in the accessibility name (`name` field); the truncation hypothesis was dropped and the extractor now takes the longer of text/name - know-how (a plausible cause was accepted before the other carrier of the same fact was checked).
- [detour] m1/failures.md#F-047 / #F-048 - closing `qualifier` into a closed enum was measured first and **abandoned**: 10 divergent real values including rate expressions (`3.4% +`, `+`, `每 GB`×72); tightening "会误伤现有语料", so it was deferred to a decision and `list` stayed an engine-hardcoded temporary token with the gap registered - policy candidate (measure the whole corpus before closing a vocabulary; a written deferral beats breaking real data).
- [detour] m1/failures.md#F-089 (S0–S5 summary) + .scratch/decisions-for-user.md#D-1 - the planned `_macro` with identity expansion was abandoned after the user rejected it ("听着非常像黑魔法") and asked for something Schema-like; the replacement (closed type registry, `_composite`, per-slot schema, missing required slot = type error) adds checkability while "不增加任何表达力", and the project's own framing is "收紧而非放宽" - policy candidate (to close a language, prefer a mechanism that adds zero expression and only adds checkability; validate it against real data before enforcing).
- [pitfall] m1/failures.md#F-094 + #F-090 / #F-091 - enforcing the new schema rejected 0/8 real `form` pages; the causes were partly the schema's own over-rigidity (`form.submit` required exactly one `submit` while real filter panels apply instantly) and partly a validator defect that failed to recognise an already-declared slot shape; the recorded rule is "不许为凑数硬套 schema", and two single-row `table` families stayed unmigrated until a warning channel existed - policy candidate (when a new invariant rejects every real instance, the invariant is on trial first).
- [project rule] m1/failures.md#F-091.b - "没人看到的警告 = 变相的错误": the designed `W_COMPOSITE_ROWS_TOO_FEW` had no output channel, so instances were left unmigrated rather than silently accepted; the fix added a `warnings` array plus a pipeline summary that does not affect the exit code - policy candidate (non-blocking findings need a visible surface and must be summarised by the gate).
- [project rule] docs/design-basis.md:119 - "无逃生口 ⇒ 覆盖声明不能靠临时加原语灌水。（原句'证明强度 = 语法限制强度'是错的：空语言限制最强、覆盖为零。）" and "覆盖只在冻结的语言快照上声明；冻结后新增原语，之前的通过作废" - policy candidate (bind a coverage claim to a frozen revision of what it measures; a later change invalidates the claim instead of silently extending it).
- [project rule] docs/design-basis.md:138 and :145-147 - the oracle must be independent of the layout it judges ("布局只是输出，不能当基准"), thresholds must land on numbers, failures must be listed, the hold-out set never participates in design, and coverage is computed only on the curated exemplar set; `m1/failures.md#文本-judge-的能力边界` adds that the harness judge has no vision, so visual main/secondary judgement is human-only and the record must separate "人（视觉）" from "agent（文本）" verdicts - policy candidate (state each checker's capability boundary and label every verdict with who produced it).

## Top 5 by value

1. [pitfall] m1/failures.md#F-040 - a validator whose root call was deleted by an unrelated diff stayed green for weeks; the general policy needs "no gate without a counterexample that fails it, shipped as a self-test", since this turned an entire proof tier into a vacuous claim.
2. [pitfall] m1/failures.md#F-078 - fidelity measured model↔render and never model↔capture, so wrong numbers sat inside a 1.00 gate; any claim of provenance or "verbatim from source" must be checked against the source, not against internal consistency.
3. [pitfall] m1/failures.md#F-052 + #F-054 - the metric's scope was silently narrow (excluded values) and its matcher was blind (substring), and both were only fixed by making the denominator tighten-only and giving the check a defect it must catch; measurement honesty needs a stated owner and a falsification case.
4. [pitfall] m1/failures.md#F-070 - the PNGs the user actually judged were older than the HTML, so a real fix read as "nothing changed"; the human-facing derived artefact must be hash-bound to the revision it shows, which also forces idempotent writers.
5. [pitfall] .scratch/active-work.md#编排事故 - `git add -A` while another writer was running swept in-flight work into a commit whose label no longer matched its content; staging is shared state, so a commit must name explicit paths while any sibling writer is alive.
