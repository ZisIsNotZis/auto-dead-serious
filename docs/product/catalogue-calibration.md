# Rating calibration
Status: disputed
Authored product-discussion record; user statements and agent proposals are separate. The existing assessment inputs remain inspectable, but their creativity/usefulness numbers are disputed rather than publication-ready.
## User-stated judgement
Creativity is about whether someone would think to attempt the thing, why they would do it, the common-sense and state-of-the-art practice, our practice, whether and why it is better, and the philosophy behind it; the judgement ranges from trivial or one-more-step to interesting or an unexpectedly great idea.
The owner places agentic-animation high because it embodies a reusable set of primitives/practice rather than a random output: actual story-script format, text as timing for action/word synchronization, automatic asynchronous and pre/post behavior, reusable assets and failure prevention by construction. These are user-attributed design claims, not independently verified safety or correctness guarantees.
The owner places auto_maintain_bench above aps because of the trials and specialized craft required to make a tiny model work reasonably without mistakes; aps still embodies substantial thought in a generalized industry-independent model with swappable engines, but is below agentic-animation.
The owner sees horse_video and huaqiang_video as low-creativity random videos that modern LLMs can produce without much reusable practice; hyperframaker's ComfyUI/Node-RED-like multimodal/plugin composition is at most around aps and probably lower in underlying thought, despite a fun result.
For current usefulness, the owner regards agentic-animation as one of the most useful existing projects because he has already made videos with it; he does not regard the two standalone videos as useful tools. aps still needs benchmark/verification before production use; even auto_maintain_bench's best known outcome is not something he would automatically put into production.
## Root error in the current ratings
Agent diagnosis: the assessment rewarded inspectable implementation, a coherent artifact and an interesting-sounding combination as creativity/usefulness. Those are evidence of work or maturity, not sufficient evidence of a non-obvious contribution or a worthwhile achieved outcome for the intended user.
Agent diagnosis: requiring exceptional independent validation for endpoint scores and using broadly shared middle anchors compressed diverse projects into 2–3. Additional decimal places would add apparent precision without resolving the judgement.
The shared policy already requires matching the observation to the real question; this is a local rubric/execution repair, not grounds to add a universal rule.
## Proposed creativity rubric
Provisional agent proposal: use an anchored integer 0–10 judgement of the non-obvious, consequential contribution relative to ordinary practice and a named relevant alternative; no numerical rescore is authorized by this proposal alone.
Proposed anchors: 0 trivial/unmodified repetition; 1–2 cosmetic or obvious extension; 3–4 competent adaptation with limited conceptual surprise; 5–6 a non-obvious abstraction or problem-specific design that removes a consequential difficulty; 7–8 a strong reusable insight/practice that eliminates recurring failure modes; 9–10 an unusually powerful new way of working within the declared problem, with a precise differentiator and convincing evidence, not a requirement to invent an unprecedented world-wide category.
For each rating, explain the pain, ordinary solution, closest relevant alternative, design departure, why that departure follows from the constraints, which failure it removes, what transfers and what remains a claim. Interestingness, non-obviousness and demonstrated advantage are named separately before the judgement; hours spent and code volume are not direct rewards.
Tie handling proposal: compare tied projects head-to-head on the same question and record a defensible ordering only when the evidence distinguishes them; preserve genuine ties and unknowns rather than manufacture one-point differences. Evidence confidence and novelty-search coverage remain separate from the rating.
## Proposed usefulness rubric
Provisional agent proposal: current usefulness rates a worthwhile achieved outcome for a named user in an actual workflow, with frequency, importance and advantage over the simplest adequate alternative; producing an artifact or passing tests does not itself make it useful.
A self-use project does not need production deployment to be useful; production readiness is judged only where that is the promised use. A research benchmark's usefulness is the decisions or trustworthy evidence it enables, not whether its tested agents are safe to deploy. Standalone creative output is not scored as a reusable production tool merely because a rendered file exists.
Expected usefulness remains conditional on the agreed finished scope, with delivery likelihood and confidence separately recorded; intended future audience or impact is never invented to inflate it.
