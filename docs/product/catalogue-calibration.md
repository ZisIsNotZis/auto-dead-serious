# Rating calibration
Status: existing ratings disputed; the nine-judgement representation approved. This authored product-discussion record owns the owner's calibration testimony and corrections; ../method/catalogue.md owns implemented anchors, schema and evidence conventions.
## User-stated judgement
Creativity is about whether someone would think to attempt the thing, why they would do it, the common-sense and state-of-the-art practice, our practice, whether and why it is better, and the philosophy behind it; the judgement ranges from trivial or one-more-step to interesting or an unexpectedly great idea.
The owner places agentic-animation high because it embodies reusable primitives/practice rather than a random output: actual story-script format, text as timing for action/word synchronization, automatic asynchronous and pre/post behavior, reusable assets and failure prevention by construction. These are user-attributed design claims, not independently verified safety or correctness guarantees.
The owner places auto_maintain_bench above aps because of the trials and specialized craft required to make a tiny model work reasonably without mistakes; aps still embodies substantial thought in a generalized industry-independent model with swappable engines, but is below agentic-animation.
The owner sees horse_video and huaqiang_video as low-creativity random videos that modern LLMs can produce without much reusable practice; hyperframaker's ComfyUI/Node-RED-like multimodal/plugin composition is at most around aps and probably lower in underlying thought, despite a fun result.
For current usefulness, the owner regards agentic-animation as one of the most useful existing projects because he has already made videos with it; he does not regard the two standalone videos as useful tools. aps still needs benchmark/verification before production use; even auto_maintain_bench's best known outcome is not something he would automatically put into production.
## Root error in the current ratings
Agent diagnosis: the assessment rewarded inspectable implementation, a coherent artifact and an interesting-sounding combination as creativity/usefulness. Those are evidence of work or maturity, not sufficient evidence of a non-obvious contribution or a worthwhile achieved outcome for the intended user.
Agent diagnosis: requiring exceptional independent validation for endpoint scores and using broadly shared middle anchors compressed diverse projects into 2–3. Additional decimal places would add apparent precision without resolving the judgement.
The shared policy already requires matching the observation to the real question; this is a local rubric/execution repair, not grounds to add a universal rule.
## Approved representation
The approved representation separates non-obviousness, depth of thought, transferability, current product value, conditional-future product value, current contribution value, conditional-future contribution value, technical feasibility and delivery feasibility. The first seven use justified integer 0–10 estimates with plausible bounds or unknown. Feasibility uses justified subjective probability ranges only with explicit constraints and horizon, otherwise unknown.
A project card links to contribution cards. A contribution may be a transferable practice/insight, an internal module or an output; project inside value references a contribution source and does not duplicate its ratings. A failed or low-value product can contain a valuable transferable contribution.
Human and AI effort are separate descriptive quantities with provenance or unknown, never inferred from Git. Maturity, evidence strength, audience, tags, delivery form, requirements, reuse/extraction burden and limitations remain separate from judgement. Preserve source evidence and distinguish owner testimony from independent observation.
There is no total or artificial tie-break. Conditional-future value is conditional on completing the stated scope and does not include delivery probability. Existing assessment drafts remain disputed until WP3 imports a complete replacement; WP1 does not rescore and a partial replacement is not M1 delivery.
## Calibration examples and limits
The owner’s examples above are calibration testimony, not independently verified results or automatic replacement ratings. Agentic-animation, auto_maintain_bench, aps, hyperframaker, horse_video and huaqiang_video should be checked against their root-pinned sources in WP2. Do not force all projects into a bell curve, infer global novelty from bounded public comparisons, or turn owner testimony into independent evidence.
