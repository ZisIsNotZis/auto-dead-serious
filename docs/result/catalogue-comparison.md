Authored parent investigation report; local evidence and revision bindings are owned by catalogue-pilot.md and catalogue-calibration.md in this directory, and membership criteria by ../product/catalogue.md.
## Question and method
Question: do public alternatives undermine the proposed differentiation of the seven assessed projects, and do the user-selected high-quality examples expose a scoring defect?
Producer: parent agent using web_search_ds and web_fetch_ds on 2026-10-19; local-input parent revision f54666f21f50b7cceffc907efd4646208f6586ab; child revision and dirty-state limits are cited in the two investigator reports rather than remeasured here.
Interpretation criterion: documented capability overlap establishes a relevant comparator, not equivalent user experience, copied implementation, lack of value or historical priority; a positive distinction is a hypothesis unless the same task is exercised on both projects.
Scope: ten search requests and ten fetch attempts, including two failed Hugging Face fetches; eight public pages successfully opened, with excerpts below; official Hugging Face indexed page content was recovered through targeted search. No installs, child code/tests, visual review, model calls or live experiments were executed or inspected by the parent.
Source quality: public project README claims and author abstracts are first-party descriptions, not independently reproduced performance or feature verification. Search rank and publisher identity do not establish truth. Repository pages are mutable, not pinned public-code snapshots; this is bounded landscape comparison, not a novelty audit or causal attribution.
## hfget
Local evidence: catalogue-pilot.md, heading “hfget — personal Hugging Face GGUF selector/downloader”.
Public evidence: https://huggingface.co/docs/huggingface_hub/v1.30.0/package_reference/cli and https://huggingface.co/docs/huggingface_hub/en/package_reference/cli were returned with page text by targeted search; excerpts include “perform a dry run without actually downloading the file” and examples combining hf download with include/exclude and local-dir.
Fetch limits: https://huggingface.co/docs/huggingface_hub/guides/cli and https://huggingface.co/docs/huggingface_hub/guides/download returned tool HTTP 404 failures; these failures establish retrieval limits, not absence of official documentation or features.
Inference: generic downloading, filtering and preview are already documented upstream; hfget's defensible contribution must be opinionated repository/quantization/companion selection, not merely downloading or dry-run support. No recovered evidence establishes that upstream reproduces its complete selection policy; absence of such evidence does not prove exclusivity.
Membership recommendation: unresolved rather than remove; a small tool can earn usefulness through a meaningful recurring workflow improvement without a large implementation. Compare selected filenames and required user decisions on a safe fixture before deciding this route.
## Suzhou Drive
Local evidence: catalogue-pilot.md, heading “drive — Suzhou offline browser driving simulator”.
Public evidence: https://github.com/BrendanH18/jev_fsd opened successfully; its README describes “real neighbourhoods from OpenStreetMap,” browser play, player or AI driving, offline map data, lanes, weather and traffic. It also explicitly says hosted decision/LLM endpoints have only mock-server testing, a useful limit on its own claims.
Inference: this is a substantially closer comparator than the pilot's CARLA/SUMO category references; “OSM roads plus browser driving” is not a sufficient distinctiveness claim. Suzhou-specific data and navigation behavior may justify a local experience, but a different city alone does not establish a general advantage. Neither relative visual quality nor feature quality was tested.
Membership recommendation: substantial-work route remains supported by the local report regardless of category overlap; useful-without-merely-repeating route remains conditional on a concrete user advantage. No evidence establishes copied work or chronology.
## dojo
Local evidence: catalogue-calibration.md, heading “`dojo` — provenance-aware ideas and discussions in a local CLI/SQLite POC”.
Public evidence: https://github.com/argdown/argdown opened successfully; excerpts describe “analyzing complex argumentation,” premise-conclusion structures, Markdown integration and exported argument maps. Its public primer surfaced in search also represents support and attack relations.
Inference: structured claims and objections are established capabilities, so those alone do not establish novelty. dojo's strongest candidate distinction is the stateful, sourced lifecycle of claims across conversations, with explicit uncertainty and disagreement, rather than producing an argument diagram. The current CLI/store does not demonstrate the envisioned chat-first network.
Membership recommendation: retain on substantial-work evidence; creativity is a plausible composition-level judgement, not proof of research novelty. Demonstrate a real objection surviving discussion without becoming false agreement before claiming the intended knowledge workflow works.
## VibeOS
Local evidence: catalogue-calibration.md, heading “`vibeos` — browser OS that stages generation of missing app surfaces”.
Public evidence: https://github.com/HeyPuter/puter opened successfully; excerpts describe a self-hostable internet computer with apps, app publishing, AI/storage services and an AI Builder link. https://github.com/stackblitz-labs/bolt.diy opened successfully; excerpts describe building full-stack apps in a browser with a code editor, preview, model-provider selection, snapshot restoration and deployment.
Inference: neither browser desktops nor AI app creation alone is a defensible novelty claim. VibeOS's candidate distinction is the runtime-triggered cache miss, scoped artifact staging/validation/publication and resumption of the initiating action. The two comparator READMEs do not establish equivalent transaction/resume semantics, but absence from a README does not prove absence from either implementation.
Membership recommendation: retain on substantive runtime/harness evidence; strong candidate product story, not established superiority, secure execution or reliable generated-app quality. A real cache-miss workflow plus failed-publication recovery is the discriminating next demonstration.
## agentic-animation
Local evidence: catalogue-calibration.md, heading “`agentic-animation` — deterministic local story-to-animation toolkit”.
Public evidence: https://github.com/motion-canvas/motion-canvas opened successfully; excerpts describe a TypeScript generator-based animation library and real-time editor, designed for vector animations synchronized with voice-overs. https://github.com/Hmzbo/animation-skills opened successfully; excerpts describe agent-operated code-driven animation rendered to MP4 using several engines. Its README includes both working-skill and stub descriptions, so broad engine coverage is not independently established by the label list.
Inference: “agents create deterministic video from code” is not enough differentiation by itself. Candidate value is the structured story-to-performance pipeline, constrained puppet motion and replaceable media adapters as one authoring workflow. Existing general-purpose engines do not by themselves establish an equivalent workflow; no same-story output/editing comparison or film review was performed.
Membership recommendation: retain on substantial-work evidence; strongest recorded end-to-end product slice in this selected local sample according to the investigator, not a parent-verified visual-quality ranking. A new, representative story and normal-size visual review would discriminate general utility from curated demos.
## causalmlp
Local evidence: catalogue-pilot.md, heading “causalmlp — causal hidden-unit prefix MLP experiment”.
Public evidence: https://arxiv.org/abs/2205.13147 opened successfully; Matryoshka Representation Learning, v4 dated 2024-02-08, explicitly proposes representations at multiple capacities and says its code/models are open-sourced. Excerpt: “allows a single embedding to adapt to the computational constraints of downstream tasks.” The paper abstract's speed/accuracy numbers are author claims and are not used to rank local projects.
Search surfaced a public implementation mirror at https://github.com/howird/representation-learning with nested representation sizes and a shared efficient head; that mirror was not fetched or independently traced to the original authors, so it is a lead rather than verified implementation equivalence.
Inference: useful nested capacity/prefix representations are established prior-art territory. The local cumulative-mean output mechanism is not shown equivalent to MRL by this bounded abstract-level comparison; exact contribution remains unresolved. The local metric mismatch reported in catalogue-pilot.md independently blocks its current headline accuracy comparison and is not repaired by locating related work.
Membership recommendation: retain as an inspectable substantial research attempt; do not promote it as a novel or successful efficient-inference method on present evidence.
## PathLM
Local evidence: catalogue-calibration.md, heading “`pathlm` — research map and experimental workbench for LM compute paths”; committed results only, no mutable experiment output.
Public evidence: https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.18855 opened successfully; Delta Attention Residuals, submitted 2026-05-13, begins “Attention Residuals replace standard additive residual connections with learned softmax attention over previous layer outputs.” The fetched abstract was truncated after its redundancy/routing-collapse discussion, so no later mechanism/performance claim is adopted from it.
A search for evaluation-framework alternatives surfaced a relevant primary repository discussion, https://github.com/EleutherAI/lm-evaluation-harness/pull/4384, and a secondary framework explanation; neither was fetched within the page budget. No mature-framework feature comparison or novelty claim for the research catalogue format is established from these search leads.

PathLM should explain its question, baseline and measured local result, not imply that a research map or every mechanism is new. Full mechanism correspondence, paper chronology versus local work, independent replication and research-framework differentiation remain unestablished.
Membership recommendation: retain on substantial-work and bounded research-evidence merits; do not infer favorable current outcomes or public deployment readiness. A paper covering the same question is a baseline obligation, not an automatic reason to discard local research.
## Calibration findings
Observation from the local reports: all four user-selected candidates have substantive implementation/experiment evidence, but their most distinctive product or research proposition is not equivalent to a mature current deliverable.
Parent inference: high-quality potential, high-quality implementation, valuable research evidence and showcase readiness must remain distinct judgements; their conjunction is not required by the user's OR membership rule.
Proposed correction: expected usefulness means value conditional on achieving the agreed finished scope, matching the user's original question; delivery likelihood and evidence confidence must be stated separately rather than silently depressing this score because work is unfinished or archived. Existing numeric forecasts in both investigator reports remain provisional and are not recalculated here without a consistent per-project finished scope.
Proposed correction: creativity reasons should discuss both novelty and interestingness, as the user requested; exact global novelty is unknown after bounded search, but that does not prevent a clearly labelled judgement about an interesting synthesis.
Proposed correction: keep substantial work as an evidenced membership route, not a proxy for project size, tests or commercial success; a flawed reported experiment can retain value as an honest attempt while its unsupported conclusion is excluded from the showcase.
Proposed correction: attach evidence confidence and inspected/executed status to ratings; 0 requires relevant evidence of no value/behavior, while missing evidence is unknown. Do not adopt one generic “maturity” anchor for all five independent aspects.

Completed: bounded public-source comparison for all seven projects, including closer comparators for Drive, dojo, VibeOS and animation; source excerpts and inferential limits are recorded above. The original investigator's missing-web gate is addressed at this bounded documentary-comparison level, not as an exhaustive search or functional comparison.

Public framework comparison for PathLM remains a search lead rather than a fetched primary comparison. The whole numeric rubric is not yet calibrated enough for a trusted leaderboard.
Recommendation: retain the six projects with substantial-work support; leave hfget's membership unresolved pending its small workflow comparison. No removal candidate has been established by this sample. Next work would fix aspect-specific anchors and conditional expected-usefulness semantics before broader scoring; full-collection investigation, child repairs, removals and README generation still require approval.
