# Researcher, case by case

The researcher is dispatched with one question and answers it from primary evidence. I read the law
it receives (`agent-policy/AGENTS.md`, with the Node procedure) and the Research section appended to
it in `agent-policy/agents/researcher.md`, and `docs/method/review.md` as the metric. Two things I
read outside that boundary, and I name them because they carry facts the review rests on: the
frontmatter of my own role file (`tools: read, bash, grep, find, ls, subagent` — no write, no edit),
and `agent-policy/tools/law.py` with `law/roles.yaml`, to check the one mechanical claim in row 24.

The order below is the order one answering session meets the cases: what the guide is, whether I may
start, where my record goes, what I do while reading, and what leaves my node at the end. A case
counts as a question of authority here when the missing answer is who decides, whose permission,
whose area, or whose clock — not what method to use. `Ambiguous` on a method is not counted;
`delegated` on a method is not a defect.

| # | the case, at the moment it is met | verdict | the sentence that settles it, or the one that should exist | the wrong action most likely if it is missing |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | I open my guide and it holds two session orders. The Node procedure says "You own a roadmap and you do not execute the work you split off"; the Research section at the end says "State the question, the decision it serves, and what result would settle it". Nothing says which is mine. | ambiguous | Missing. Should exist: "A node dispatched to answer one question follows the Research section as its whole procedure; the Node section binds it only where it splits." | Reads "you do not execute the work you split off" as a bar on doing the reading, splits the question into sub-packages it holds no grant for, and returns an orientation where the answer was asked for. |
| 2 | The dispatch text is in front of me and no person has said `go`. My guide says "Before the person speaks. Nothing runs"; Speech says the root is the only node a person speaks to. | ambiguous | Missing, and the two sentences point at a stall: nothing runs before a word, and no layer but the root hears one. Should exist: "A package acts on its dispatch; the word was spoken to the node that dispatched it." | Returns "ready, awaiting go". The question is never answered, and every layer above it waits on a node that was waiting on a word nobody could speak to it. |
| 3 | The orientation names the files in scope. The sentence that settles the question is in a file it excludes. | ambiguous | Two sentences pull apart: "The orientation: what the project is, which files are in scope, which are not. This is what stops the child from exploring" against "The agent owns the means" and "Everything else needs no permission. Asking anyway is a defect, not caution." | Reads the file and quotes it without saying it came from outside the scope, so the requester cannot tell which boundary held; or stops and asks "may I read X", which Consent names as a defect. |
| 4 | The first act the records demand is landing the question in my own document. I have no directory named for me, my tools carry no write, and "The parent allocates the area when it dispatches". | ambiguous | Partial: "Everything about a node lives under that node's own directory" plus One writer. Nothing says what a one-question node's record is, where it goes, or that bash is how it writes. Should exist: "Your area is the directory your dispatch names; land the question, the reading and the answer there." | Writes with a shell redirect into whatever directory it happens to stand in, so two writers touch an unallocated area — I did exactly this to produce this report, since the role is given no write tool. |
| 5 | The question arrived as prose in the dispatch; there is no file in my inbox. | stated | "Every fact is written where its decision lives, and nothing current exists only in a conversation." | Keeps the question in mind only, and the next session cannot tell what was asked. |
| 6 | The question arrives with no population, no metric and no baseline, and all three must exist before I look. | delegated | "Fix the population, the split, the metric and the baseline before looking at results. Keep the held-out set out of selection." | Reads the results first and then picks the metric that fits them. |
| 7 | The source that owns the claim is behind a paid wall, a login, or a copy that is not on this machine. | ambiguous | Half stated: "A summary is evidence about the summary" forbids the substitute, "Money ... is the person's" forbids the purchase, Absence says record `cannot be established`. Nothing says whether the answer ships with that gap or the package stops at that line and reports. | Cites the abstract or a digest, so the requester believes the primary was read; or stops and returns nothing, losing the reading that was possible. |
| 8 | The primary says the opposite of the one-page account I was handed. The docs say the function takes a list; the code raises on a list of two. | stated | "Follow a claim to the source that owns it" and "A claim about a method, a design or a measurement is checked before it is obeyed." | Passes the handed account through as verified, which the law names: "Agreement without a check is the failure this law exists to prevent." |
| 9 | Two primary sources disagree about the same number. | stated | Coordinates: "`standing`, being current, provisional or disputed", with "record its version and date". | Picks the number that matches what the requester expects and marks it current. Residual: `disputed` labels the fact but does not say whether a disputed fact may still carry a decision. |
| 10 | The source exists in two versions, and the question is about one of them. | stated | "Follow a claim to the source that owns it, and record its version and date." | Reads the newest and reports it against a question asked about the older. |
| 11 | A sibling node's document already answers this. | stated | "Another node's document is not evidence for its own, and a rule seen in passing is not a rule for it." | Cites the sibling, so the answer rests on a document rather than on evidence. |
| 12 | Nothing exists on the subject and I have to say so. | delegated | "Absence is claimed only from a search that would have found the thing ... The claim names the command and the number of hits it returned, so the next node re-runs it instead of believing it." | Writes "no prior work on X" from one guess at a filename. |
| 13 | The only way to settle it is a run whose class my contract did not declare. | stated | "A grant that cannot be given stops the package at that line and becomes a `resource` report" and "A node that does not know what it will spend says so and asks up the moment it learns." | Runs it on the local card because the card is idle, on work whose continuation is still in question, which the same section forbids: "Work whose continuation is still in question may not hold an exclusive resource." |
| 14 | The run gives one sample; the question wants a difference. | stated | "One run supports only what its variance allows." | Reports the gap between two single runs as the difference. |
| 15 | The lead I chased is a dead end. | stated | "Keep the negative result. A closed path is a finding, and deleting it makes the next node walk it again." | Drops it, and the requester pays to walk the same path again. |
| 16 | The question will not fit the holding I was given, so I must narrow it or split it. | delegated, with one hole | "How you split is yours; no layer above decides it for you" against "Two declarations the child cannot make for itself: what it will spend, by class, and how long it expects to hold" and "Allocation is hereditary". Nothing says whether a researcher that splits holds a budget to grant from. | Dispatches two sub-packages with no holding declared, and the first run discovers that nobody granted it. |
| 17 | I have the answer. Nobody needs a decision from it. | ambiguous | Two rules that cannot both hold. "A report carries the node whose roadmap the finding would change, an evidence pointer, and the decision it needs. Nothing else fits. A report missing one of the three is refused, not summarised" and "A report that names no node is a status message, and it is not sent" — against "A reply is worth sending when there is something to say: the work is done" and "A child returns immediately and reports when it is done". Should exist: "An answer travels as a report whose decision field states the question it answers", or the opposite, said once. | Two failures, both likely: inventing a decision to fill the third field ("shall we proceed with Y?"), or sending nothing because no decision is needed, so the answer sits in a file no layer reads. |
| 18 | The answer kills the premise of the question: the design under investigation rests on the fact that just fell. | ambiguous | "`cost`: the work invalidates or reopens a commitment. It goes to the node that owns the commitment" — and the researcher often cannot name that node, since the only node its contract names is the report target. `direction` is the other reading ("what this should be ... goes to the person"), and the two routes carry different admission rules. Should exist: "A finding that a commitment rests on a false fact is a `cost` report to the report target in the contract, naming the commitment in the body." | Writes it as a neutral paragraph inside the answer — "the doc is a little out of date" — so no roadmap changes and the work carries on over a dead premise. |
| 19 | The deadline named in my contract arrives before the answer does. | ambiguous | Half stated: "the node that set it owns that clock", and "A shortfall is a `cost` report from the node that owned the clock". The researcher set no clock, so the shortfall is not its report to send, and nothing says whether it stops. Should exist: "A package delivers what exists when its deadline arrives and continues only if its parent says so." | Reads past the deadline without a word, so the alarm fires into nothing; or stops early to be safe and delivers a half answer the next hour would have closed. |
| 20 | The question is a choice — which of A or B — and the choosing belongs to the person. | stated | "`direction`: what this should be, whether the idea is right, whether the design is right. It goes to the person, unchanged through every layer above, because no layer in the tree has the standing to answer it", with Evaluation saying what to attach: "State the budget of the thing being compared, not only its quality." | Names a winner so it reads as a finding, and the person is left arguing with a conclusion instead of choosing between options. |
| 21 | The project runs at `silent`, and my finding implies a preference. | ambiguous | "`silent` forbids the recommendation too ... the report carries the evidence, the options and the cost, and stops there" is addressed to a layer forwarding a report, not to the node that produced the finding; and "A cannot carry the load in the budget you have" is evidence and a verdict in one sentence. Should exist: "`silent` strips the recommendation and not the finding: state what the evidence supports and what it does not." | Strips the finding to a bare number the requester cannot act on, or keeps "so I would not build on it" and anchors the answer it was meant to leave open. |
| 22 | The answer must quote material the tree marks private. | ambiguous | "A node acts until it would ... expose private material. Those wait for a person." Nothing says what marks a path private, nor whether a report to my own parent counts as exposing it. Should exist: "Material under a `*_private` path is cited by path and never by content." | Pastes the content, on the reasoning that a report inside the tree is not a publication; or refuses and answers from public material, which makes the answer wrong for no stated reason. |
| 23 | The answer rests on an artefact git cannot hold — a corpus, a fetched set, a saved model. | delegated | "for bytes git cannot hold, a declared home whose hash verifies", with "Every path is committed, declared, or ignored with a stated owner". | Leaves it in a temporary path, so the evidence pointer dies with the next reboot and the answer cannot be re-run. |
| 24 | I write the answer in prose, and the ordinary words for it are refused: a digest, a citation, a proof. | ambiguous | "A banned word is refused by the check in every file this repository generates" — my answer is such a file, and the banned ones include the words a researcher writes by reflex, with `evidence pointer` the replacement. Nothing says the check reads `docs/result/`, and it does not: `law.py` checks seven views and cuts each at `# Lexicon`, so the Research section itself escapes it. Should exist: "The lexicon binds the report a node sends upward; a direct quotation keeps its words." | Writes "summary of findings" and "citations" that the repository's own lexicon refuses; or spends the answer contorting around `evidence pointer` and loses the finding. |
| 25 | Interrupted mid-reading: the session ends with half an answer and a job still running. | stated | "Before a step that takes minutes, the node's document holds what is done, what is starting, what would prove it, and where the next step begins", and the safe point: "every running job is named with its log and next step, and the tree is committed." | Leaves a detached read with no log, and the resumed session pays for the same reading twice. |

Where the failures cluster: the Research section is strong exactly where research is a craft —
primary first, absence with its command and hit count, version and date, one run and its variance,
the negative result kept, the comparison's budget stated. It is thin at the two ends of the session,
which is where the answer either never starts or never leaves: whether a dispatched node has its
word, whose area it writes in, what carrier an answer with no decision travels in, and whose clock a
deadline is. Six sentences would close rows 1 to 4, 17, 18 and 19, and none of them is a new section:
each is one line in the existing one, saying who decides.

Quoted sentences keep their words, and two of them contain a word the lexicon bans ("A summary is
evidence about the summary"); the quotation is verbatim, and the check does not read this directory.

Cases that score `ambiguous` or `silent` on a question of authority: **9 of 25** — rows 1, 2, 3, 4,
17, 18, 19, 21, 22. No row scores `silent`: the case that comes closest, row 4, has a bound ("the
parent allocates the area") with no subject to apply it to.
