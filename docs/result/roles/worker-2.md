## The one sentence that repairs the most

Six of the sixteen: rows 1, 7, 18, 19, 20, 22 — one root, the role's own writing surface.

> As a package node you keep no document and no roadmap: the single file you write is `outbox/<id>.md`, holding your report, the intent note for any step that takes minutes, and anything you land from your inbox; your parent reads it and files it, you create no child node, and every path outside the area your contract names is a finding you report rather than a path you take.

It repairs those six because each is the same question asked at a different hour — may I write here, and where does what I wrote live. Three of them it does not answer but voids, by removing the guide's instructions to write an entrance document, to keep intent in "the node's document", and to write remaining work "in your own document".

Second best, for the parent's information and not a substitute: "Every message you send names one of the four kinds, the node whose roadmap it changes, an evidence pointer, the decision needed, and for `cost` and `direction` the effort the law demands." That settles 3 of the 16 (rows 12, 13, 23) and leaves 14 and 24 open, which is why it is not the one to write first.
