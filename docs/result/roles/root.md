# Root — role review

I read `agent-policy/AGENTS.md` (the law and the Node guide) and `docs/method/review.md` (the metric), and nothing else. This report takes the root's position at the moment a person types `go` and follows the session forward. The cases below are in the order a session meets them, not grouped by topic, so a case's neighbours are the cases it collides with in a real turn.

The root is the only node that speaks to a person, so every case where the law says "the person" is a case the root must resolve alone, unaided by any layer above it. That is why the authority misses below cluster where they do: money, a child's milestone, the root's own milestone, and the path back down to the node that asked. The law is consistent about the route up and thin about the route down and about the top.

The `axis` column separates a question of **who decides** from a question of **how to proceed**, because the metric allows the second to be delegated and forbids the first from being left open.

## What the cases say together

The route up is complete: `resource`, `cost`, `direction` and `contract` each name their destination, and forwarding rules are stated for every layer. The three places the documents thin out are the three places only the root stands.

The first is the top. R25 is the sharpest case in this review: the law requires the root to report its milestone and simultaneously forbids the only form that report could take, because a report must name a node above it and above the root there is none. Every other node's milestone report is well specified; the root's is specified out of existence.

The second is money. R9 and R19 are the same ambiguity twice: "the root grants a container a budget" reads as authority, while money is "the person's at `recommend` and at `decide` alike." The root is the only node for which these two sentences are both about itself.

The third is the path back down. R21 has no rule at all. The law carefully builds the upward route as a file in an outbox, and says nothing about how an answer descends, which is the one act the root performs more often than any other.

R12, R11, R15, R22 and R28 are authority gaps of the same shape: the documents describe the child's behaviour and the person's ownership, and leave the moment between them to the root's judgement.

The procedure cases that score silent, R7, R26, R27 and R31, are not dangerous on their own. They are dangerous because each one fails quietly: nothing reports that the root chose an order, kept a queue in the transcript, wrote a long answer, or skipped a check.

## Count

**12 cases score `ambiguous` or `silent` on a question of authority**: R3, R5, R9, R11, R12, R15, R19, R21, R22, R23, R25, R28.

Within them, the four I would repair before any other work: R25 (the root's milestone report has no legal form), R9 (the root can grant money to itself), R21 (the answer has no downward path), and R12 (a child sets the root's milestone).
