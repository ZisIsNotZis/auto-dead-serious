## Proposed product scope
Represent each currently registered project with a project card and linked contribution/subproduct cards; count only consequential independently reusable contributions, not every module or design choice, and keep each assessment in one source home.
Use nine separate judgements: non-obviousness, depth of thought, transferability, current product value, conditional-future product value, current contribution value, conditional-future contribution value, technical feasibility and delivery feasibility. Conditional future value does not include delivery probability, and no weighted total decides membership.
Use anchored integer 0–10 judgements with justified plausible ranges for value/insight assessments; technical/delivery feasibility use separately justified subjective probability ranges only where constraints and delivery horizon can be stated, otherwise unknown. Do not fabricate a mean, standard deviation, hours, AI usage, confidence interval or success probability.
Record human and AI effort as separate descriptive quantities with source/provenance; record maturity/progress, evidence strength, audience, domain/contribution tags, delivery form, requirements, use/reuse burden and material limitations separately from value. Unknown effort stays unknown rather than being inferred from Git history.
The front view stays compact: purpose, strongest contribution, current product value, current inside value, conditional potential, feasibility and maturity; detailed score reasons and evidence remain reachable without storing duplicate manual summaries.
