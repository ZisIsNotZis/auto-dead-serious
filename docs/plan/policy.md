# Policy

The repository's product is the policy in AGENTS.yaml, rendered to AGENTS.md and checked by `python3 render_policy.py`. This is the plan for that product.

## Status

- AGENTS.yaml is the only source and AGENTS.md is its generated view; `python3 render_policy.py --check` passes and runs on every commit through `.githooks/pre-commit`, enabled once per clone by `git config core.hooksPath .githooks`, a command no README states yet.
- The naming rule sits beside `one_name`, where naming is the subject, and its own example of the banned shape is still instantiated in its text.
- No document cites a ticket, an issue or a session.

## Amendment one, agreed, not yet applied

Rulings: a claim that no longer describes the world dies while retraction, a negative finding and evidence survive; `upkeep.archive` and the compatibility clause die; `roles.inversion` carries the end/means split, one round of argument, then execution with the dissent recorded, and urgency buys one line of explanation; the superseded catalogue work records are killed now.

Nodes: `ssot.kill`, `upkeep.supersede`, `record.plan_tree`, `roles.inversion`, `discussion.corrected`, `self_improvement.intake`.
Edits: `discussion.divergence` also covers the terse or heated message, `outdated` loses the compatibility clause and its instantiated example, and the naming rule loses its guidance-only mark once the lint refuses the shape.

## Work packages

- `1.structural-move` — done. `plain_name` sits beside `one_name`, wording unchanged, and the render differs by that one line alone.
- `2.amendment` — the six nodes, the three deletions, the two edits. Acceptance: the check passes and the kill rule is exercised by writing one banned token and watching the check refuse it.
- `3.names` — the lint over document text and file names, then the rename of 135 tokens in `docs/` and the root README, one in `.pi`, at their generators rather than in generated output. Acceptance: zero hits, check passes, the catalogue scripts still reproduce their artefacts.
- `4.inheritance` — `render_policy.py` chains a parent source into a scope's render and refuses a duplicate key, because a scope today cannot inherit: `pathlm/AGENTS.md` is 190 hand-written lines with no source and no inherited law.
- `5.extraction` — proposed. The policy becomes its own repository that a scope consumes at a pinned commit, and the render's banner names the canonical URL, the source revision and the issue URL so any copy found anywhere points home.
