# Policy

The repository's product is the policy in AGENTS.yaml, rendered to AGENTS.md and checked by `python3 render_policy.py`. This is the plan for that product.

## Status

- AGENTS.yaml is the only source; AGENTS.md is its generated view, and `python3 render_policy.py --check` passes.
- The workspace holds neither .scratch nor .tmp, and no document cites a ticket, an issue or a session.

## Proposed amendment, not yet confirmed
Owner's four demands, in his words: SSOT is a kill and not a patch, not a changelog, no deprecated trace in any physical form; a later decision beats an earlier one and every agreed thing is one node in the plan tree rather than fuzziness; discussion is a symmetric agreement in which the agent owns means and quality and does not obey a wrong instruction; a correction is diagnosed as a class before it is patched, or the improvement loop starves at its first step.
Proposed nodes: `ssot.kill` for the carrier list, the same-act deletion and a zero-hit grep as completion evidence, with compatibility admitted only as a declared current consumer; `upkeep.supersede` for a decision as a state change that replaces its predecessor instead of annotating it; `record.plan_tree` for one node with owner and acceptance, or an explicit non-goal; `roles.inversion` for the end belonging to him and the means to the agent, with one round of argument and then execution with the dissent recorded; `discussion.corrected` for class, mechanism and rung before the instance.
Proposed deletions in the same act: `upkeep.archive`, because @outdated already says git history is the archive; the compatibility clause in @outdated; the urgency clause in @roles.product_owner.align, because urgency buys one line of explanation and never silence; the in-code deviation note in @migrate_on_touch, which becomes a plan node.
Proposed extension instead of a new node: @discussion.divergence also covers the terse or heated message, where the act the agent infers is never a mass irreversible one.
